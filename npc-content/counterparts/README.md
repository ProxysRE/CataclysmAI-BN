# Reuse suitable BN content

The user's 2026-10-18 policy permits differences in statistics, appearance, materials and ordinary behavior when BN already has a suitable counterpart. Preserve the role of a location or quest; adapt genuinely missing content rather than reproducing incidental DDA systems. Ask the user about uncertain replacements that would change that role.

`bn_counterparts.py` records 15 same-namespace mappings used in the adapted nursing-home and retirement-community layer. They reuse BN medical zombies and their own evolution, training equipment, lockers, clothes, common loot and soil. All target IDs are checked against the complete pinned BN catalog; relevant BN definitions are archived here and checked against the pinned checkout in Windows preflight. Both source and adapted layers remain reviewable.

The farm adapter additionally performs schema-aware reuse of bike helmets (including the former modular-helmet group), stock rock and existing industrial carpet. Its unique functional content remains separate where ordinary BN substitutes would lose that function.

`LOOT_SOURCE_REVIEW.json` preserves the exact relevant BN item-factory excerpts. Root `container-item` is not loaded on a group. `bn_loot_containers.py` instead creates a container item with a supported `contents-group` modifier, with original selection probabilities and quantities in a separate contents group. The correction applies to farm supply packages and karting cash registers, and is covered by native content/capacity tests and package guards.

These helpers do not make the whole eldercare locations playable. Their terminal interaction bridge and remaining unmatched dependencies still require work. Native farm packaging/generation and the new cash-register behavior await Windows runtime results.
